A few useful answers

Why won’t a station play?

A stream may have moved, gone offline, be region-restricted or be incompatible with your current network. Try another network or station, then refresh the directory. A recent directory check does not guarantee availability in every location.

Can I listen offline?

Live playback needs an internet connection. Cached listings and saved station information can be browsed offline. The current release does not record broadcasts, download audio or offer offline programmes.

Where are favorites and history stored?

They are stored on your device. Clearing listening history preserves favorites. Device backup settings and uninstalling the app may affect local data.

Are song recognition or captions available?

The initial release does not offer song recognition, live captions or translation. Development experiments are not released feature promises.

Why do stations and counts change?

The directory is community-maintained, and broadcasters and stream addresses change. AirAtlas does not guarantee fixed station counts or partnerships with every listed broadcaster.

Station corrections & removal

If you represent a broadcaster or a rights holder and need to correct or remove a listing, email the station name, its Radio Browser UUID or public website, proof of your relationship to the station, and the correction/removal requested. Do not send confidential contracts unless a secure channel has been agreed.

AirAtlas does not host or rebroadcast the streams. Changes to the community directory may also require contacting Radio Browser. We will review requests within the scope we control. A directory listing does not imply partnership or ownership of third-party content.
