A few useful answers

How do I import stations?

Open Settings → App Settings → Import Stations, choose a UTF-8 JSON file and confirm. You can also manually add a stream address from My Stations in Library. Use files and addresses you are entitled to access.

What file format is supported?

The JSON root is an array. Each station needs stationuuid (a unique string), name and url or url_resolved (an HTTP/HTTPS address). Country, language, categories, artwork and coordinates are optional. Files up to 150 MB and 100,000 entries are supported; a valid-entry count is shown before import.

Why won’t a station play?

An address may expire, move or restrict access. Check the address in your file, connectivity and provider conditions; import an updated file when needed.

Does clearing the directory remove favorites?

No. Clearing the imported directory on this device keeps favorites, manually added stations and history, and current playback can continue. Import full directories separately on each device; supported personal records sync through iCloud.

Can I listen offline or identify songs?

Live playback needs internet access; saved station information can be browsed offline. Optional song identification depends on the audio and service availability. Captions and translation are currently unavailable.

Tool scope, content rights & requests

AirAtlas is a playback and personal-library tool for user-imported stations. You choose files and manually added addresses; playback connects directly to the corresponding audio service. Importing does not publish your entries to other users.

Follow provider terms and access restrictions. Importing, saving favorites or generating artwork grants no rights to programmes, music, logos or other materials. The app does not offer broadcast downloads or bypass access controls.

Rights holders or authorized representatives can contact the email above with the relevant name or URL, material concerned, authority and requested action. Do not send passwords or private tokens. We review matters within our control; changes to source services require their providers. See Terms of use.
