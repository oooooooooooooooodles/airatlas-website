Privacy policy

What stays on your device, what connects to the internet, and the choices you have.

Scope and operator

This policy covers the AirAtlas iPhone and iPad app and this website, provided by LIYINGJIE. AirAtlas is a playback tool for stations you import yourself. It supports JSON import, manually added stream addresses, browsing and searching your personal library, favorites, recent playback and song identification.

Privacy enquiries: 911306393@qq.com.

Information on your device

Station names, stream addresses, country or region, language, categories, artwork addresses and optional coordinates in your file are parsed and stored on your device for browsing, searching and playback. Import replaces the directory on this device while preserving favorites, manually added stations and history. The full imported directory stays on this device and does not participate in the app’s iCloud sync; import it separately on other devices.

Personal library records, recognition history, listening statistics, goals and appearance preferences are stored locally. Reusable artwork and other resources may be cached. Addresses can contain private parameters: choose files and links carefully and do not send passwords or full access tokens in feedback.

Personal iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, supported personal library records, recognition history, listening statistics and preferences sync through your Apple CloudKit private database to devices using the same iCloud account. Deletions of those personal records also sync; timing depends on connectivity and Apple’s scheduling. Replacing or clearing an imported directory affects only this device, not imported directories on other devices.

Full imported directories, artwork caches, audio and GPS location are excluded from this sync. Manage app access in system iCloud settings. Sync and system device backup are separate.

Playback and network connections

File parsing and browsing or searching imported station data take place on your device. Playback connects to the audio service addressed by your imported file or manually added URL. Artwork loads from its image address. Those providers receive your IP address and normal request information, and apply their own logging practices. AirAtlas does not use a developer-operated backend to receive your full import file or personal library.

Live playback requires internet access and may incur mobile data charges. Opening external websites, music apps, store listings or sharing tools is subject to the relevant service’s data practices.

Audio and song identification

The app does not use your microphone or offer user-facing broadcast recording, audio downloads or offline programme saving. Playback uses temporary buffering. When you request song identification, the app processes current audio and sends an audio signature to Apple’s Shazam service, which receives the signature and normal connection information. Results may be inaccurate or unmatched.

Recognition history can be managed in your library and syncs when iCloud is available. Captions and translation are currently unavailable. The app integrates no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK and sends no usage analytics to an AirAtlas-operated analytics service.

Regional information and location

Regional information can use a station’s imported country, region or coordinates for weather, time zones and Wikipedia content. Providers receive query parameters, IP addresses and normal connection information. Station coordinates are not necessarily your GPS location. Data and technology licences are listed in the app’s acknowledgements.

When In Use location permission is requested only when you use Current Location. Location is used on your device to find nearby stations and position the globe, not for background tracking or upload to an AirAtlas-operated server. Revoke access in system Settings.

Retention and your choices

Import stations or clear the imported directory from Settings → App Settings. Clearing the directory preserves favorites, manually added stations and history, and current playback can continue. Manage personal records separately in Library; supported deletion operations sync to other devices. Clear reusable caches in Storage settings; the system may also remove caches.

Uninstalling generally removes local app data but does not automatically delete iCloud records. System backup restoration or later sync may restore some personal records. Local clearing does not delete third-party server logs. Contact the operator about access, correction or deletion requests.

Website, contact and hosting

This static website embeds no analytics scripts, advertising, third-party fonts or online forms. Email links use your mail client. When you contact us, we receive what you choose to send and use it to handle your request, without automatically subscribing you to marketing.

GitHub Pages processes hosting requests, including IP addresses and normal connection information, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Younger users

AirAtlas is for a general audience, not specifically designed for children. Imported links and live audio are controlled by their providers. Parents or guardians should help younger users choose appropriate content.
