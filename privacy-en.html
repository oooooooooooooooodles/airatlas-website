Privacy policy

What stays on your device, what connects to the internet, and the choices you have.

Scope and operator

This policy covers the AirAtlas iOS internet-radio app and this privacy/support website, provided by 李英杰. The initial release offers station discovery, search, playback, favorites, listening history and custom stations. It does not offer account registration, advertising SDKs, audio recording, transcription, translation or cloud sync.

Contact 911306393@qq.com for privacy questions and requests.

Information stored on your device

Favorites, history, custom station addresses, selected region, appearance preferences, directory results and generated artwork caches are stored locally. AirAtlas currently has no developer-operated backend receiving this library. Custom addresses may contain access parameters; save only trusted addresses you are entitled to use.

Depending on your Apple settings, local app data may be included in device backups. This is separate from an AirAtlas cloud-sync service.

Network connections and third parties

  • Directory and search: requests go to Radio Browser nodes and may include your search text, country, category, pagination parameters and app-version identifier. These services receive your IP address and normal request information.
  • Playback: your device connects directly to the broadcaster or streaming provider, which receives normal connection information, including your IP address. Broadcasts may contain the broadcaster’s own advertising.
  • Station clicks: when playback starts, the app makes a best-effort station-click request to Radio Browser for directory listening statistics.
  • Logos and external services: station images supplied by the directory load from image providers. If you choose to open a station website, maps or sharing tools, those services process the subsequent interaction under their own terms.

Third parties determine whether and for how long their server logs are retained. Local library storage does not mean third parties receive no network information. See Radio Browser for directory information.

Permissions, analytics and audio

The current distribution version does not use your microphone, record or upload radio audio for recognition, integrate advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs, or send analytics to an AirAtlas-operated analytics service. Background audio is used to continue playback while the app is backgrounded or your device is locked.

Development-only transcription and song-recognition experiments are excluded from the current Release build. Any future feature will have updated disclosures and applicable permission requests.

Retention and your choices

You can clear directory caches and listening history in Settings, remove favorites, and delete custom stations. Clearing history preserves favorites. Generated artwork is a reproducible cache subject to size-based eviction; the system may also remove caches.

Uninstalling generally removes local app data, while restoring a device backup may restore backed-up data. Local clear buttons do not delete third-party server logs. Contact the operator about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests; requests involving independent broadcasters are subject to their separate processing.

This website and hosting

This static website does not embed analytics scripts, advertising, third-party fonts or online forms. Email links use your email client. Your email provider processes the message, and the operator receives the information you choose to send.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes hosting requests under the GitHub Privacy Statement. A static site does not prevent the host from receiving an IP address.

Younger users

AirAtlas is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. Live broadcasts are independent third-party content and may change. Younger users should use the app with guidance from a parent or guardian; directory inclusion is not a guarantee that every programme is suitable for every age.
