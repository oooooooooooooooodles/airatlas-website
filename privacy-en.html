Privacy policy

What stays on your device, what connects to the internet, and the choices you have.

Scope and operator

This policy covers the AirAtlas iOS internet-radio app and this privacy/support website, provided by LIYINGJIE. The initial release offers station discovery, search, playback, favorites, listening history and custom stations. It does not offer account registration, advertising SDKs or audio recording. Personal radio-library data syncs automatically when iCloud is available; app access can be managed in system Settings. Song recognition is user-initiated and depends on stream format and service availability. Captions and translation are currently unavailable.

Contact 911306393@qq.com for privacy questions and requests.

Information stored on your device

Favorites, history, custom station addresses, listening duration, selected region, appearance preferences, directory results and generated artwork caches are stored locally. AirAtlas currently has no developer-operated backend receiving this library. Custom addresses may contain access parameters; save only trusted addresses you are entitled to use.

When iCloud is available, favorites, custom stations, recent playback, recognition history, daily and cumulative listening time, listening goals, and region/appearance settings are stored in your personal Apple CloudKit private database. Directory caches, audio, location, captions and transcripts are not uploaded. Deletions and clearing actions also propagate when sync is available; timing depends on connectivity and Apple service scheduling. System device backup is separate from this sync.

Network connections and third parties

  • Directory and search: requests go to Radio Browser nodes and may include your search text, country, category, pagination parameters and app-version identifier. These services receive your IP address and normal request information.
  • Playback: your device connects directly to the broadcaster or streaming provider, which receives normal connection information, including your IP address. Broadcasts may contain the broadcaster’s own advertising.
  • Station clicks: when playback starts, the app makes a best-effort station-click request to Radio Browser for directory listening statistics.
  • Logos and external services: station images supplied by the directory load from image providers. If you choose to open a station website, maps or sharing tools, those services process the subsequent interaction under their own terms.

Third parties determine whether and for how long their server logs are retained. Local library storage does not mean third parties receive no network information. See Radio Browser for directory information.

Regional information and external services

Opening station regional information may query weather, time zones and Wikipedia using the station’s region, country or coordinates. Providers receive query parameters, IP addresses and normal connection information. Station coordinates are not necessarily your GPS location. Opening a music platform or its store listing is subject to that platform’s data practices.

Permissions, analytics and audio

The app does not use your microphone, save radio recordings, integrate advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs, or send analytics to an AirAtlas-operated analytics service. Background audio is used to continue playback while the app is backgrounded or your device is locked.

When you enable song recognition, the app opens an additional connection to the station, analyzes audio in memory and sends an audio signature to Apple’s Shazam service for matching. Shazam receives that signature and normal connection information. Recognition history includes song details, station information and recognition time; it can be deleted in the library and syncs with iCloud. Recognition may use additional data. Captions and translation are currently unavailable and ordinary playback does not initiate transcription or translation. Third-party terms and privacy policies apply.

Optional current location

Only when you tap the globe location button does the app request When In Use permission and a one-time location fix. Approximate location is supported. Coordinates remain in device memory to find the nearest station and position the globe; AirAtlas does not store them in history or caches, upload them to its servers or the radio directory, or track location in the background. You can revoke access in iOS Settings.

Retention and your choices

You can clear directory caches and listening history in Settings, remove favorites, and delete custom stations. Clearing history preserves favorites. When iCloud is enabled, history clearing, favorite changes and custom-station deletions also sync. Disabling sync does not delete existing iCloud data; manage it in system iCloud storage settings. Generated artwork is a reproducible cache subject to size-based eviction; the system may also remove caches.

Uninstalling generally removes local app data, while restoring a device backup may restore backed-up data. Local clear buttons do not delete third-party server logs. Contact the operator about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests; requests involving independent broadcasters are subject to their separate processing.

This website and hosting

This static website does not embed analytics scripts, advertising, third-party fonts or online forms. Email links use your email client. Your email provider processes the message, and the operator receives the information you choose to send.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes hosting requests under the GitHub Privacy Statement. A static site does not prevent the host from receiving an IP address.

Younger users

AirAtlas is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. Live broadcasts are independent third-party content and may change. Younger users should use the app with guidance from a parent or guardian; directory inclusion is not a guarantee that every programme is suitable for every age.
