Playback and identification

Your device connects directly to the selected audio source. AirAtlas does not host or relay audio on developer-operated servers or offer user-facing broadcast recording, audio downloads or offline programme saving. Live playback needs internet access and may incur carrier data charges. File completeness, address validity, access restrictions and source quality affect use. Availability and station counts depend on your supplied information and source services; no particular address is guaranteed to remain available. Retain your original file before replacing an import.

Personal records and iCloud

Synced deletions may affect other devices. Import full directories separately on each device. Clearing an imported directory removes only that device’s import and preserves personal records. Sync is not an independent backup and may be delayed. See the privacy policy.
